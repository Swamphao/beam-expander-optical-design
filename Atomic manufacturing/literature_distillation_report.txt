### Title
Two-dimensional superconductivity in the cuprates revealed by atomic-layer-by-layer molecular beam epitaxy

### Publication Year
2016

### DOI
10.1088/0953-2048/29/10/103001

### Journal
Superconductor Science and Technology

### Ten Keywords
high-temperature superconductivity; two-dimensional superconductivity; cuprates; molecular beam epitaxy; atomic-layer-by-layer; superlattices; interface superconductivity; superconductor-insulator transition; thin films

### Authors
A T Bollinger, I Božović

### Contact Author
I Božović (bozovic@bnl.gov)

### Affiliations
1. Condensed Matter Physics and Materials Science Division, Brookhaven National Laboratory, Upton NY 11973, USA
2. Applied Physics Department, Yale University, New Haven CT 06520, USA

### Abstract
Various electronic phases displayed by cuprates that exhibit high temperature superconductivity continue to attract much interest. We provide a short review of several experiments that we have performed aimed at investigating the superconducting state in these compounds. Measurements on single-phase films, bilayers, and superlattices all point to the conclusion that the high-temperature superconductivity (HTS) in these materials is an essentially quasi-two dimensional phenomenon. With proper control over the film growth, HTS can exist in a single copper oxide plane with the critical temperatures as high as that achieved in the bulk samples.

### Objective
利用原子层分子束外延（ALL-MBE）技术研究铜氧化物高温超导体的超导态本质维度特性，系统验证高温超导是否为准二维现象。通过单层膜、双层膜和超晶格的结构化生长，精确控制铜氧化层的数量和间距，探究超导态的维度依赖性。

### Novelty
通过 ALL-MBE 技术实现原子级精度的铜氧化物薄膜生长，系统性地证明了高温超导本质上是一个准二维现象。关键发现：即使超导层被非超导层分隔高达 60Å，临界温度 Tc 仍不下降，明确证明超导态局域于单个铜氧平面内。

### Method
使用自制 ALL-MBE 系统在超高真空条件下生长铜氧化物薄膜，生长速率约每分钟一个单原子层，沉积精度达百分之一原子单层。实时 RHEED 监控、臭氧氧化和原子吸收光谱监测分子束流量。生长单组分膜、S-S'-S 超晶格和 S-I-S 超晶格，随后进行退火优化氧含量，通过四探针电阻和磁化率测量超导转变温度。

### Performance Metrics
Bi2Sr2CuO6（2201）薄膜 Tc≈15K，Bi2Sr2CaCu2O8（2212）薄膜 Tc≈85K。超晶格测试中，即使 2212 超导层被 2201 非超导层分隔达 60Å（5:1 超晶格），Tc 仍保持在 85K 不变。LSCO/LCO S-I-S 超晶格中，仅 1 个晶胞厚度的 LSCO 层仍保持 Tc≈30K 的超导性。薄膜表面粗糙度达 1-2Å 原子级平整。

### Objective
本条目旨在定义扫描隧道显微镜（STM）技术，阐明其作为一种原子级成像技术的核心原理。目标是提供STM技术的简明概念描述，帮助读者理解其基本工作机制。

### Novelty
本文是Qeios开放同行评审平台上的一个定义条目，将STM技术收录为权威的科学术语。其新颖性在于以开放、可访问的方式提供标准化的技术定义，促进科学知识的共享和传播。

### Method

### Performance Metrics

### Objective
本文旨在综述二维材料在原子和近原子尺度上的增材制造技术进展。目标是探索能够实现二维材料位置选择性和层数精确控制的多种原子级增材制造工艺，推动其在电子、生物传感和纳米机电系统中的应用。

### Novelty
本文是首篇系统综述二维材料原子级增材制造（ACSM）的文献，将传统减材制造与原子级增材制造进行对比分析。文章创新性地梳理了位点选择性CVD、区域选择性ALD、电沉积、激光辅助合成、印刷方法和原子层堆叠等多种技术路线，并提出了实现原子级增材制造的两大核心条件：平面位置控制与层数分辨率。

### Objective
本文旨在提出材料-结构-性能一体化增材制造（MSPI-AM）的宏观概念，为激光金属增材制造面临的科学和技术挑战提供系统性解决方案。目标是通过并行设计多材料布局、创新结构和相应打印工艺，主动实现高性能和多功能性。

### Novelty
本文创新性地提出了"将正确材料打印在正确位置"和"为独特功能打印独特结构"两大方法论理念。与传统的"设计-制造-验证"串行模式不同，MSPI-AM强调材料、结构、工艺和性能四个核心要素的并行集成，从纳米/微米尺度的微观结构到宏观尺度的构件性能实现跨尺度协调。

### Method
MSPI-AM涵盖三大层次：（1）纳米/微米尺度的材料开发，包括纳米复合材料、原位复合材料和梯度材料的设计与打印；（2）介观尺度的工艺监控，涉及激光与打印物质相互作用的实时监测；（3）宏观尺度的结构控制和性能验证，包括一体化拓扑优化结构、仿生结构和多级细胞结构的设计。

### Performance Metrics
MSPI-AM实现了从单材料打印到多材料/多功能设计和制造的跨越。激光定向能量沉积（LDED）的沉积层厚度通常不超过1 mm，建造速度可达约11.5 cm³/min。激光粉末床熔合（LPBF）的粉末层厚度通常低于100 μm，最大建造体积边长可达800 mm以上。该技术已成功应用于航空、航天、汽车制造和能源生产等高端工业领域。

### Abstract
This article presents the three paradigms of manufacturing advancement: Manufacturing I, craft-based manufacturing by hand, as in the Stone, Bronze, and Iron Ages, in which manufacturing precision was at the millimeter scale; Manufacturing II, precision-controllable manufacturing using machinery whereby the scales of material removal, migration, and addition were reduced from millimeters to micrometers and even nanometers; and Manufacturing III, manufacturing objectives and processes are directly focused on atoms, spanning the macro through the micro- to the nanoscale, whereby manufacturing is based on removal, migration, and addition at the atomic scale, namely, atomic and close-to-atomic scale manufacturing (ACSM). A typical characteristic of ACSM is that energy directly impacts the atom to be removed, migrated, and added. ACSM, as the next generation of manufacturing technology, will be employed to build atomic-scale features for required functions and performance with the capacity of mass production. It will be the leading development trend in manufacturing technology and will play a significant role in the manufacture of high-end components and future products.

### Abstract
Atomic and close-to-atomic scale manufacturing (ACSM) represents techniques for manufacturing high-end products in various fields, including future-generation computing, communication, energy, and medical devices and materials. In this paper, the theoretical boundary between ACSM and classical manufacturing is identified after a thorough discussion of quantum mechanics and their effects on manufacturing. The physical origins of atomic interactions and energy beams-matter interactions are revealed from the point view of quantum mechanics. The mechanisms that dominate several key ACSM processes are introduced, and a current numerical study on these processes is reviewed. ### Abstract
This work reports on the development of STM lithography for Atomically Precise Advanced Manufacturing (APAM) targeting ultra-precise 2D bipolar devices. The research covers automated STM patterning with atomic precision from the single-pixel scale (0.768 nm) up to full STM scanner range. Key developments include real-time correction of creep, hysteresis, and thermal drift using the ZyVector controller, precursor chemistry for both donor (PH3) and acceptor (BCl3) dopants, and fabrication of p-i-n junctions with variable gap widths (0-10 pixels = 0-7.68 nm). Simulations using the Charon device modeling code predict strong gap-width-dependent device behavior, including negative differential conductance that diminishes when the gap exceeds 4 nm.

### Objective
发展 STM 光刻技术用于原子级精确先进制造（APAM），实现超高精度二维双极器件的制造。核心目标是开发可同时放置施主（P）和受主（B）掺杂原子的自动化 STM 系统，实现原子级精度的 p-n 结制造。

### Novelty
ZyVector STM 光刻控制系统实现了从单像素（0.768nm）到全扫描范围的原子级精确图案化，实时修正蠕变、迟滞和热漂移误差。dI/dZ 和 dI/dV 多变量成像技术同时与形貌成像结合，提供掺杂剂图案的额外电子对比度，简化了图案对准。开发了一套从施主到受主的双极器件制造全流程。

### Method
(1) 使用 ZyVector 控制系统进行 STM 光刻，以 SVG 图案绘制掺杂剂位置；(2) 先写入 n 型图案，用 PH3 饱和，热退火掺入 P 原子；(3) 重新定位并写入 p 型图案，用 BCl3 饱和 B 原子，与 P 区域对准；(4) dI/dZ 和 dI/dV 成像辅助对准，精度达二聚体行分辨率；(5) 制造变间隙宽度的 p-i-n 结（0, 3, 10 像素 = 0, 2.31, 7.68 nm）；(6) 使用 Sandia 实验室的 Charon 开源器件建模代码进行仿真。

### Abstract
The advance of modern technology requires the ability to produce ever-shrinking devices, in some cases reaching atomic scales or relying on quantum effects. This demand for quantum devices requires fabrication on the atomic scale to guarantee precise control of crystalline layers, active dopant placement, and to avoid or understand atomic-scale defects. However, current techniques for nanoscale fabrication in the semiconductor industry lack the capability to fabricate on the atomic scale. The current state of the art in atomic fabrication includes the positioning of single atoms and surface lithography using scanning tunnelling microscopy (STM) and atomic force microscopy (AFM) on two-dimensional surfaces, but an active quantum device would require sub-surface dopant positioning. Scanning transmission electron microscopy (STEM) has recently been demonstrated as a technique for the epitaxial growth of material at a crystalline-amorphous (CA) interface. Here we show the ability to crystallize, amorphize, remove material, and direct dopants, which form important steps toward quantum device fabrication.

### Objective
开发基于 STEM 的电子束诱导技术，实现硅中纳米/原子级精确制造，包括晶体生长、非晶化、材料去除和掺杂剂定位。解决半导体工业当前缺乏原子级制造能力的问题，为量子器件制造提供关键技术。

### Novelty
首次利用 STEM 作为原子级制造工具，在硅的晶体-非晶（CA）界面上实现外延生长、非晶化、钻孔和掺杂剂定向移动。相比传统 STM/AFM 局限于表面的原子操纵，STEM 方法可操纵表面下（sub-surface）的掺杂剂，这是量子器件制造的关键需求。开发了基于傅里叶变换振幅反馈的原子层控制算法。

### Method
使用 STEM 原子级精确探针，将电子束聚焦到原子尺度并扫描样品。将束扫描从晶体基质进入非晶区域时，在硅基质上外延生长晶体硅。增加束流可将晶体硅转化为非晶硅或钻孔移除材料。开发反馈控制系统：沿 CA 界面平行扫描，通过计算 STEM 信号傅里叶变换振幅确定结晶度，当达到预期转变时自动推进一个原子层。使用高浓度 Bi 掺杂剂样品，直接观察电子束扫描如何沿多个硅晶体轴垂直移动掺杂剂。

### Performance Metrics
STEM 探针聚焦至原子尺度，实现单原子层级别的外延生长控制。傅里叶变换反馈系统实现原子层步进精度。电子束可在多个硅晶体轴方向上移动 Bi 掺杂剂。束流强度可调以控制结晶、非晶化和材料去除三种模式。相比传统 STM 表面操纵，此方法可直接控制表面下掺杂剂位置。

### Abstract
Structural materials in nature exhibit remarkable designs with building blocks, often hierarchically arranged from the nanometer to the macroscopic length scales. We report on the structural properties of biosilica observed in the hexactinellid sponge Euplectella sp. Consolidated, nanometer-scaled silica spheres are arranged in well-defined microscopic concentric rings glued together by organic matrix to form laminated spicules. The assembly of these spicules into bundles, effected by the laminated silica-based cement, results in the formation of a macroscopic cylindrical square-lattice cagelike structure reinforced by diagonal ridges. ### Abstract

Here we introduce a deep learning-based strategy that autonomously executes multi-step, bond-selective transformations. Our system integrates computer vision for molecular recognition, neural networks for bond-state classification, and deep reinforcement learning for closed-loop optimization of activation parameters. As a proof of concept, we demonstrate the selective dissociation of C-Br bonds in a tetra-brominated porphyrin on Au(111). Importantly, the approach extends beyond single-bond events, enabling programmed multi-step sequences including four distinct pathways with high fidelity. By advancing from isolated, human-directed manipulations to fully autonomous, data-driven reaction control, this platform establishes a paradigm for intelligent single-molecule chemistry. ### Abstract
Although continued investments in nanotechnology are made, atomically precise manufacturing (APM) to date is still regarded as speculative technology. APM, also known as molecular manufacturing, is a token example of a converging technology, has great potential to impact and be affected by other emerging technologies, such as artificial intelligence, biotechnology, and ICT. The development of APM thus can have drastic global impacts depending on how it is designed and used. This article argues that the ethical issues that arise from APM - as both a standalone technology or as a converging one - affects the roles of stakeholders in such a way as to warrant an alternate means furthering responsible innovation in APM research. This article introduces a value-based design methodology called value sensitive design (VSD) that may serve as a suitable framework to adequately cater to the values of stakeholders. Ultimately, it is concluded that VSD is a strong candidate framework for addressing the moral concerns of stakeholders during the preliminary stages of technological development. Incompatibility with the thermal and lithog- raphy process requirements for gated silicon transistor manufacturing have inhibited exploration of both how APAM can enhance CMOS performance and how transistor manufacturing steps can accelerate the discovery of new APAM device concepts. ### Objective
本文旨在提出一种概念证明，将原子精度先进制造（APAM）直接集成到CMOS晶体管制造的中间流程（MOL）中。目标是解决APAM与传统CMOS制造工艺在热预算和光刻兼容性方面的两大障碍，使APAM技术能够在晶圆厂环境中应用。

### Novelty
本文首次展示了APAM与CMOS制造工艺的直接集成方案。创新性地提出了使用离子溅射代替传统高温退火来去除表面氧化物，将APAM的热预算从约1000°C降低至约600°C。同时，用光刻兼容的介质硬掩模替代传统的氢光刻技术，实现了与标准CMOS制造流程的兼容。

### Method
采用三步APAM流程：（1）使用2.0 keV氖离子溅射30分钟去除表面氧化物，随后在550-600°C进行固相外延（SPE）退火重结晶；（2）利用介质硬掩模定义图案，选择性掺杂磷前驱体分子；（3）用外延硅封顶激活掺杂剂。将该模块插入Sandia实验室0.35 μm CMOS工艺的FEOL和BEOL之间进行验证。

### Performance Metrics
